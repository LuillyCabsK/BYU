My First Temple Web Page

For many years, members from my hometown of Popayán, Cauca had to travel approximately 15 hours to reach the Bogotá Colombia Temple. In the coming years, we will be able to attend the house of the Lord in less than 3 hours. All members in our district feel truly blessed by this blessing.

The Cali Colombia Temple will be the third temple in Colombia

The Cali Colombia Temple was announced by President Russell M. Nelson on April 4, 2021, during the Sunday afternoon session of General Conference. It will be built in the La Flora neighborhood of Cali, Valle del Cauca.[1]

[1] Cali Colombia Temple. (2025). The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. churchofjesuschristtemples.org
